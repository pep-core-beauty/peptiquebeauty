FAQ

Before you checkout

How much is shipping?

J&T: Metro Manila ₱100, Luzon ₱150, Visayas ₱200, Mindanao ₱250. Lalamove delivery fees are paid directly to the rider and are not added to your website total.

When will my order ship?

Confirmed and paid orders received before the daily cut-off may be shipped on the same day. Courier timing can still vary.

Do you accept cash on delivery?

No. Peptique Beauty PH follows a strictly payment-first policy prior to shipping.

Are reseller discounts applied here?

No. The prices shown on this website are the listed set prices. Reseller discounts are not applied on this storefront.

Is this medical advice?

No. Product information on this storefront is for catalog and ordering purposes only and is not medical advice. Use products only as appropriate and with guidance from a qualified healthcare professional where applicable.
